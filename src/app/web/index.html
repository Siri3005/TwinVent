WHAT IS TWINVENT?

TwinVent is an offline research prototype that replays test-lung breaths and simulates how a changed input may alter predicted airway pressure.

Current validation uses a controlled test-lung benchmark. Patient outcomes and clinical ventilator settings are not represented.
